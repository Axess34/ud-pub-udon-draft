About us · เกี่ยวกับเรา

A quiet courtyard just off the street.

Step in from Phonpisai Road and the city falls away: dark timber, warm decks, still water and tall trees overhead. UD PUB is a relaxed spot to sit down with friends, order a round and stay a while.

Pull up a chair on the deck by the pond, or settle into the lounge inside.

  • Cold beer & drinks with friends
  • Open-air courtyard and an indoor lounge
  • Celebrations through the year — Songkran, Christmas & New Year
